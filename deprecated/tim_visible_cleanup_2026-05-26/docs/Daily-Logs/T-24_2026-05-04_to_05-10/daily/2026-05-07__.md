
## Dataset bag recording mode

Added `--record-dataset` to `tools/start_live_stack.sh`.

Purpose:

- record reusable drone-view RGB footage
- support offline replay on the Pi
- support detector evaluation and possible future fine-tuning
- keep TIM telemetry available as reference metadata

`artifacts/bags/datasets/`

Offline replay rule:

Replay only source camera topics when testing a new detector, tracker, or TIM version:

`ros2 bag play <dataset_bag> --topics /camera/image_raw /camera/fps`

Do not replay old `/detections`, `/tracks`, or `/target_memory` when generating new outputs.
